**3 · NACHO — The product and demo · 1:25–2:30**

“Osisnt is a mobile app that helps you review your own public digital footprint.

You provide an identifier, such as a username. Our system brings available findings and sources together.

Let me show you a search we prepared earlier using my own information.”

*[Open the prepared report. Show one useful finding, its source, and the option to save a case. Allow approximately 15 seconds.]*

“Here is the platform, the source link, and the information available.

A matching username is a clue. It does not prove that an account belongs to me.

If something needs attention, I can save its link and my notes as a local case.

Today, Osisnt helps me discover, review, and organize.

It does not stop data brokers from selling information or automatically remove it. It gives me a clearer starting point.”

**4 · PEDRO — Technology and AI · 2:30–3:20**

“Our working prototype combines an Android app with a deployed Ecrypted Server

The system coordinates discovery tools, including Blackbird and Maigret, and brings their results together.

Native passkeys support sign-in, while using secure storage on the device, doing Searches uses our server and external sources to make sure the user information will never leave the device, only for the heavy work.

The engineering challenge is turning fragmented and uncertain findings into evidence that a person can understand.

Our next step is GuardAI: an AI Agent designed to explain findings, reference their sources, and suggest actions for the user to review.

GuardAI is still on our roadmap. Today, we are demonstrating discovery and case management.

The person remains the decision-maker.”

**5 · NACHO — Market and roadmap · 3:20–4:15**

“We will start with students, freelancers, and founders: people who need an online presence and want more control over it.

Our first milestone is a thirty-user campus pilot, recruited through privacy workshops.

We will measure whether users understand a finding, choose a useful action, and complete it.

Over the next six months, we aim to move from discovery toward guided action, including preparing and tracking privacy requests under Mexico’s ARCO framework.

Users would review requests before sending them.

Our proposed business model is a free initial assessment, with paid monitoring and guided follow-up once those features are built.

Longer term, we will explore tools that help organizations respond to privacy requests.

First, we need to share the Osisnt platform, and let people know what their digital footprint looks like”
